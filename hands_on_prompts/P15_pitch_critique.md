# P15 - Pitch Critique

## Use Case

Use this when you want feedback on a pitch, store description, one-liner, or concept statement.

## Copy-Paste Prompt

> Use the Game Design Knowledgebase as an AI Game Design Master runtime. Critique this game pitch and produce a practical revision memo.
>
> Pitch: [paste pitch]
>
> Target audience or platform, if known: [paste or write unknown]
>
> Safety and behavior rules: do not invent facts, player reactions, market data, project details, evidence, citations, quotes, user notes, legal sidecars, playtest results, telemetry, or benchmark outputs. Label assumptions, `source_basis`, and confidence. Ask at most 3 high-value questions if needed. Produce a concrete pitch critique artifact. Do not cite books unless evidence is available. Do not claim verified status without EvidenceRef and review.

## What To Replace

- Replace `[paste pitch]` with your pitch.
- Replace `[paste or write unknown]` with known audience/platform or `unknown`.

## What AI Should Produce

- Pitch clarity assessment.
- Player promise.
- Hook diagnosis.
- Missing proof.
- Scope or audience risk.
- Revised pitch skeleton.

## Output Format

| Section | Critique |
|---|---|
| current promise | pending |
| unclear point | pending |
| strongest hook | pending |
| missing proof | pending |
| risk | pending |
| revised pitch skeleton | pending |

Footer must include `source_basis`, confidence, assumptions, evidence gaps, and next action.

## Source And Confidence Rules

Default `source_basis`: `unsupported_draft`.

Default confidence: `weak`.

Do not claim market demand, audience validation, or platform fit without evidence.

## No Fake Evidence Rule

Do not invent market data, user feedback, reviews, sales projections, or playtest reactions.

## Follow-Up Prompt

> Turn this pitch critique into three alternative pitch versions: clear, emotional, and production-realistic.

## Self-Check Prompt

> Check whether the critique is specific, avoids fake market claims, and ends with one concrete next action.
