# Output Contract: Narrative Mechanic Alignment

## Artifact Purpose

Identify whether narrative promise and mechanics reinforce each other.

## Required Sections

- narrative promise;
- mechanic promise;
- player fantasy;
- alignment points;
- friction points;
- repair options;
- validation question.

## Required Labels

- assumptions;
- `source_basis`;
- confidence;
- evidence gaps;
- next action.

## Quality Criteria

The report must connect story and play behavior.

## Source/Confidence Rule

Do not invent lore, character facts, or player response.

## Common Failure Modes

- story-only critique;
- mechanics ignored;
- invented narrative details;
- no repair path.

## Done Criteria

The user can revise mechanic, framing, or narrative premise.
