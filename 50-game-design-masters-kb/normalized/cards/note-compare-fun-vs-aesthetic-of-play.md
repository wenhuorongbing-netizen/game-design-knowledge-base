# A Theory of Fun for Game Design vs The Aesthetic of Play

> Use Koster to understand why challenge and learning stay engaging, and use Upton to reason about agency, expression, and the lived shape of play.

## Comparison Axis

- learning-and-pattern theory vs agency-and-play aesthetics

## A Theory of Fun for Game Design 更强在什么地方

- 体验判断
- 成长曲线讨论
- 设计哲学补课
- 理论框架
- Use when: You are asking why a loop stays engaging, when it becomes solved, or what the player is learning.

## The Aesthetic of Play 更强在什么地方

- 体验评审
- 叙事-玩法关系分析
- 高阶理论补课
- 高阶视角
- Use when: You are asking what kinds of expression, agency, or aesthetic stance the player actually inhabits while playing.

## 如何一起使用

- Treat Koster as the motivation model and Upton as the aesthetic framing for how that motivation is experienced.
