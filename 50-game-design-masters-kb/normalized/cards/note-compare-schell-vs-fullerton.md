# The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses vs Game Design Workshop

> Use Schell to pressure-test direction and experience intent, then use Fullerton to convert that intent into prototype and playtest cadence.

## Comparison Axis

- high-level review lenses vs playcentric production loop

## The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses 更强在什么地方

- 高层方向收敛
- 设计评审
- 跨团队对齐
- 问题清单构建
- Use when: The team is still arguing about vision, player experience, or what the game should become.

## Game Design Workshop 更强在什么地方

- 原型推进
- 试玩流程建设
- 设计文档规范化
- 团队训练
- Use when: The team already has a direction and now needs prototype structure, playtest flow, and iteration discipline.

## 如何一起使用

- Run a lens-based review first, then turn the unresolved questions into a playcentric prototype plan.
