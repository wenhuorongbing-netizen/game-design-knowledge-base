## Practical First Trial

Recommended first trial:

1. Open `USE_THIS_FIRST.md`.
2. Open `USE_CASE_HUB.md`.
3. Choose either "I have no active project" or "I have a vague game idea."
4. Load the matching context pack.
5. Copy the matching hands-on prompt.
6. Ask AI for one artifact.
7. Check the output with `FIRST_USE_CHECKLIST.md`.
8. Record confusion points.
