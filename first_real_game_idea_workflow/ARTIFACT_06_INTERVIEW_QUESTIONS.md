## Non-Leading Question Rule

When a real prototype exists, questions should ask what the participant noticed, tried, expected, or found confusing without implying the desired answer.

## What Not To Ask

- Do not ask whether the game was fun if no prototype exists.
- Do not ask whether a specific invented feature worked.
- Do not ask participants to agree with the designer's intended fantasy.
- Do not ask about nonexistent mechanics, levels, UI, economy, story, or feedback.
- Do not ask questions that imply a correct answer.
