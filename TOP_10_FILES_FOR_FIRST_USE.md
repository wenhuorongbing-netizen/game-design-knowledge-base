## Purpose

## What Is Not In The Top 10

Not first-use:

- benchmark files;
- validation reports;
- source-governance audits;
- evidence sidecars;
- schemas;
- generated exports;
- legacy folders;
- deprecated material;
- private source folders.

These files are still important. They are just not first-use files.
