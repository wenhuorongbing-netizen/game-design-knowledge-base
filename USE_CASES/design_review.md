# Use Case: Design Review

Use this when you have a rough concept, mechanic, loop, feature, pitch, or design decision and want structured critique.

## What You Provide

- The thing to review.
- What you are worried about.
- Optional: player goal, current prototype state, or constraints.

## Copy-Paste Prompt

> Use the Game Design Knowledgebase as an AI Game Design Master runtime. Review the design below. Route it to the right master capability, select 3 to 5 lenses, choose one workflow if an artifact is useful, and produce a prioritized design review.
>
> Include: strongest design opportunity, top risks, lens findings, concrete revision suggestions, unknowns, assumptions, `source_basis`, confidence, evidence gaps, and one next action. Ask at most 3 questions only if needed.
>
> Design to review: [paste design]
>
> My concern: [paste concern]

## Expected Output

| Section | What you should get |
|---|---|
| route | Lead capability and domain. |
| selected lenses | Why each lens applies. |
| review findings | Prioritized issues, not generic advice. |
| artifact | Memo, matrix, map, checklist, or prototype question sheet. |
| revision path | Concrete next changes. |
| safety labels | assumptions, `source_basis`, confidence, evidence gaps. |

## Safety Boundary

The AI may critique design logic, but it must not invent playtest results, project facts, player reactions, or verified source claims.
