## User Reading Note Goal

The user does not need to summarize entire books first. The best next step is to write small notes that answer:

- What capability should this work improve?
- What question should the AI learn to ask?
- What artifact should the AI learn to produce?
- What claim should remain unsupported until better evidence exists?
