# 90-Day Game Design Master Plan

Date: 2026-04-29

## Purpose

This is the main capability-based learning path for building the user's game design skill and improving the AI Master Framework.

It does not summarize books. It tells the user what to study, what to practice, what notes to create, and what artifact to produce.

## 90-Day Completion Criteria

- 13 output artifacts created.
- At least 13 user-authored notes created.
- At least 10 AI master prompts tried.
- At least 5 concepts marked for evidence upgrade.
- No private source body text parsed by AI.
