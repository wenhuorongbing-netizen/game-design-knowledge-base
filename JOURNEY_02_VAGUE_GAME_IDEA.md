Date: 2026-04-30

Use this if you have a rough game idea but it is not clear yet.

## Required Input

Write your rough idea in 1 to 5 sentences.

Include:

- what the player does;
- the fantasy or mood;
- one thing you think is interesting.

## Optional Input

You may add:

- target platform;
- genre references;
- player emotion you want;
- constraints such as solo dev, tabletop, or prototype only.

## Context Pack To Load

Load [context_packs/CP02_game_idea_review.md](context_packs/CP02_game_idea_review.md).

## Copy-Paste Prompt To Use

Use [hands_on_prompts/P01_review_my_game_idea.md](hands_on_prompts/P01_review_my_game_idea.md).

If the AI says the core experience is unclear, follow with [hands_on_prompts/P02_define_core_experience.md](hands_on_prompts/P02_define_core_experience.md).

## Expected Artifact

The AI should produce:

- a one-page concept memo;
- a possible core experience;
- design pillars;
- the likely core loop;
- up to 3 missing-input questions;
- major design risks;
- one next prototype question.

## Good Output Checklist

A good output:

- makes the idea more specific;
- asks only a few important questions;
- does not invent market demand or player data;
- marks assumptions clearly;
- includes source_basis and confidence;
- gives one next action.

## Accessibility Notes

If you feel stuck, paste only one sentence. The AI should ask at most 3 questions before giving a useful draft.

Ask for a "one-page memo" instead of a long design document.

## Source/Confidence Rule

The result is a design scaffold, not proof.

Default:

- source_basis: unsupported_draft
- confidence: weak
- verified claims: none

## Common Confusion Points

- The AI can help structure your idea before evidence exists.
- A concept memo is not a promise that the design will work.
- Do not ask the AI to cite books unless evidence is available.

## Next Action

Save the one-page concept memo and ask the AI to turn it into one prototype question.
