This sequence turns study into design practice. Exercises are original KB practice scaffolds, not copied book exercises.

## Exercise Rules

- Do not ask the AI to copy exercises from books.
- Every exercise should produce an artifact.
- Every artifact should list assumptions and evidence gaps.
- If the exercise creates a claim, mark it as `unsupported_draft`, `ai_hypothesis`, or `user_interpretation` unless evidence exists.
