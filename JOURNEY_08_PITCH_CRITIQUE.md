Date: 2026-04-30

## Who This Is For

Use this if you have a short pitch and want it to be clearer, sharper, and more testable.

## When To Use It

Use this when you need a concept pitch, store-page blurb, portfolio pitch, or verbal explanation reviewed.

## Required Input

- the pitch text;
- target audience;
- what you want the pitch to accomplish.

- genre;

- comparable games if you supply them;
- current design pillars;
- known constraints.

## Expected Artifact

The AI should produce:

- pitch diagnosis;
- unclear promise or audience risks;
- sharper wording options;
- missing proof points;
- next revision;
- source_basis and confidence labels.

## Good Output Checklist

A good output:

- improves clarity without inventing features;
- separates promise, audience, and proof;
- does not invent market data;
- does not claim player appeal without evidence;
- gives a revised pitch you can test;
- includes source_basis and confidence.

## Accessibility Notes

Ask for "three pitch rewrites and one reason each" if the full critique feels too long.

Keep your input under one page for first use.

## Source/Confidence Rule

Pitch critique is draft design feedback unless you provide market, playtest, or user evidence.

Default:

- source_basis: unsupported_draft
- confidence: weak
- verified claims: none

## Common Confusion Points

- A pitch can be clearer without being proven.
- Comparable games are references, not evidence that your game will succeed.
- The AI must not invent audience demand or market validation.

## Next Action

Choose one revised pitch and ask the AI for a 3-question audience comprehension test.
