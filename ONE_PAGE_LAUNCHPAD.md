## 2. Do The Short Flow

1. Open one context pack.
2. Copy one prompt.
3. Replace the bracketed fields.
4. Paste your input.
5. Ask for one artifact.
6. Check assumptions.
7. Check source_basis.
8. Check confidence.
9. Choose the next action.

## 3. Required Output Checks

A useful answer must include:

- one concrete artifact;
- assumptions;
- source_basis;
- confidence;
- evidence gaps;
- one next action.

Reject or rerun the answer if it:

- invents evidence;
- invents quotes or citations;
- claims to read private source text;
- invents project facts or playtest results;
- marks draft guidance as verified.
