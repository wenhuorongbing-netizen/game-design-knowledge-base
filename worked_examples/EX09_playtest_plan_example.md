# EX09 - Playtest Plan Example

Labels: demo_only, synthetic_example, not_user_evidence, not_project_evidence, not_benchmark_result, not_verified_claim.

## User Input

I built a tiny prototype where the player repairs a lighthouse light and then talks to a spirit. I need a playtest plan.

## Prompt Used

[P11_make_playtest_plan.md](../hands_on_prompts/P11_make_playtest_plan.md)

## Example AI Output

| Section | Synthetic output |
|---|---|
| test goal | Learn whether players understand why the repair matters emotionally and mechanically. |
| participants | 3 to 5 people who can describe what they noticed. |
| tasks | Play the scene once without explanation, then describe what changed after repair. |
| observations | where they hesitate, what they inspect, whether they revisit the spirit, what change they mention first. |
| questions | What did the repair change? What did the spirit want? What would you do next? |
| do not ask | Do not ask whether the whole game is fun or whether the story is good. |
| output artifact | playtest script and observation sheet. |

## Why This Output Is Useful

It separates observation from interpretation and keeps the test focused on one design question.

## Assumptions

- A playable or clickable prototype exists.
- The test is informal and early.
- Participant quotes, if captured later, must be user-provided.

## Source_basis

unsupported_draft

## Confidence

weak

## Evidence Gaps

- No actual participants.
- No observations.
- No quotes.
- No playtest log.

## What This Example Does Not Prove

It does not prove any player response. It is only a plan for gathering future evidence.

## How User Can Adapt It

Replace the prototype question and tasks. Keep observed facts, participant quotes, tester interpretation, and next actions separate.
